## Current Project: AI Calendar App

I'm currently developing an AI-integrated calendar application designed specifically for students. The goal is to create a smart scheduling system that can:

### Key Features
- **Intelligent Scheduling**: Automatically suggests optimal times for different types of tasks
- **Priority Management**: Helps balance academic, personal, and professional commitments
- **Adaptive Learning**: Learns from user patterns to provide better recommendations
- **Integration Capabilities**: Works seamlessly with existing productivity tools

### Why This Project?

As a student myself, I understand the challenges of managing multiple deadlines, classes, and personal commitments. Traditional calendar apps are passive tools - they store information but don't help optimize your time. My vision is to create an active assistant that helps students make the most of their limited time.
